HT/LT Panels & Power Distribution

Comprehensive solutions for high-tension (HT) and low-tension (LT) power distribution, including the supply, installation, testing and commissioning of HT/LT panels, capacitor panels, main distribution boards (MDBs), sub-main distribution boards (SMDBs), final distribution boards and associated protection and control equipment. Works include panel positioning and assembly, cable glanding and termination, earthing connections, circuit identification, functional checks and coordination with the approved electrical drawings and project specifications.

Busducts & Rising Mains

Supply, installation, testing and commissioning of busbar trunking systems and rising mains for reliable power distribution in commercial, industrial and residential buildings. Equipment is sourced through authorised OEM channels and installed in accordance with approved general arrangement (GA) drawings, manufacturer recommendations and project specifications.

The work includes route coordination, setting out, installation of supports and hangers, busduct alignment and jointing, connection to transformers and switchgear, tap-off unit installation, earthing and bonding, and inspection of completed joints. Systems are checked for installation quality and electrical continuity before energisation, with testing and commissioning carried out in line with applicable requirements.

Wiring & Cabling

Comprehensive installation of power and lighting wiring, as well as connections from distribution boards (DBs) to equipment. Works include cable route coordination, installation of conduits and cable supports, HT and LT cable pulling, dressing, securing, tagging and identification, followed by glanding and termination at panels, DBs and equipment.

Cable sizes and routes are coordinated with approved drawings and project specifications. Before energisation, connections are inspected and checked for continuity, insulation resistance, correct identification and secure termination to support safe and reliable operation.

Critical Power & Equipment Connections

Installation and connection of UPS systems and battery banks to provide dependable backup power for critical loads. Works include electrical fit-outs for server and communication rooms, dedicated power supplies for air-conditioning units, and power connections for pumps, motors and other essential equipment.

Services cover coordination of equipment locations and power requirements, cable route preparation, installation of cable supports and containment, power and control cable laying, earthing and bonding, glanding, termination, and equipment connection. Where specified, UPS and battery works also include checking polarity, interconnections and installation clearances in line with manufacturer guidance.

Before energisation, installations are inspected and tested for insulation resistance, continuity, correct phase sequence, earthing and secure terminations. Final checks are coordinated with the equipment supplier and project team to support safe start-up and reliable operation in accordance with approved drawings and project specifications.

Earthing, Surge & Lightning Protection

Installation of earthing and protective systems to help safeguard people, buildings and electrical equipment against electric shock, fault currents, lightning and transient voltage surges. The scope includes earth pits and electrodes, earthing grids, GI and copper conductors or strips, and bonding connections for panels, equipment, cable trays and other specified metallic services.

Lightning protection works may include air terminals, down conductors, test joints and connections to the earth termination network. Surge protection devices (SPDs) can be installed at designated distribution boards and equipment points, coordinated with the electrical system and the project design.

All components are installed to approved drawings, project specifications, manufacturer recommendations and applicable standards. On completion, the system is visually inspected and tested for conductor continuity and earth resistance. Test results are documented for handover and commissioning records.

Electrical Containment Systems

Supply and installation of electrical containment to route and protect power, lighting and control cables throughout a building. The scope includes conduits installed within slabs, walls and floors; sleeves for cable and conduit penetrations; and back boxes for switches, sockets and other electrical accessories.

Cable management systems include GI cable trays, cable ladders, trunking and raceways, complete with bends, tees, reducers, couplers, covers, brackets, supports and other required fittings. Routes and containment sizes are coordinated with approved drawings, cable requirements, site conditions and other building services.

Installation includes setting out routes, fixing supports, maintaining alignment and clearances, providing access for future inspection and maintenance, and protecting cable routes at wall and floor penetrations. All works are completed in accordance with project specifications, manufacturer recommendations and applicable standards, ready for cable installation and subsequent inspection.

Lighting, Switches & Electrical Accessories

Supply, installation, testing and commissioning of lighting systems and electrical accessories for commercial, industrial and residential projects. The scope includes indoor and outdoor LED luminaires, emergency and exit lights, and decorative light fixtures approved by the client and architect. Fixture selection and positioning are coordinated with the approved drawings and project requirements to achieve the specified illumination levels (lux levels).

Services also include lighting management and control systems, standard and industrial-grade switches and socket outlets, fans and junction boxes. Industrial-grade accessories are selected to suit the required application, location and equipment rating. Installation covers setting out, mounting, wiring, circuit connections, identification and coordination with other site services.

Completed works are inspected for secure installation and correct connections. Lighting circuits, switches, sockets, controls and emergency lighting functions are tested for proper operation in accordance with approved drawings, project specifications and applicable requirements.

Electrical Testing & Commissioning

Testing and verification of electrical installations to confirm safe, reliable operation before handover. Services include insulation resistance (IR) and continuity testing, megger testing, circuit and polarity verification, panel load testing, and testing of HT/LT equipment.

Testing is carried out in accordance with approved drawings, project specifications, equipment manufacturer recommendations and applicable standards. Results are documented to support commissioning, rectify identified issues and provide records for project handover.

Fire Safety Systems

Supply, installation, testing and commissioning of systems supporting fire detection, alarm, emergency response and fire-fighting operations. The scope includes fire alarm and detection systems, emergency lighting and exit signs, dedicated power supplies for fire-fighting equipment, and gas suppression systems for LT panels, as specified in the project design.

Works include installation and connection of fire alarm panels, detectors, manual call points, sounders, visual alarm devices and associated cabling; emergency lights and exit signs; and power connections for fire pumps and other designated fire-fighting equipment. Gas suppression systems for LT panels include the specified detection and release components, control interfaces, and panel protection arrangements. Interfaces with other building systems are coordinated where required by the approved design.

All installations are carried out in accordance with approved drawings, project specifications, manufacturer recommendations and applicable local fire safety regulations. Systems are inspected and functionally tested, including verification of device operation, alarm indications, emergency lighting, suppression system interfaces and power connections. Test results and commissioning records are prepared for handover.
