Established in 2008, AAYKAY Electricals Private Limited is an ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 certified contractor specialising in electrical and fire safety services. We provide end-to-end expertise in design, supply, installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance.

Our commitment to quality, safety, responsive service and timely project delivery has earned the trust of leading multinational and Indian organisations. We have completed projects across banking, healthcare, manufacturing, data centres, IT parks, interior fit-outs, substations, commercial buildings and high-rise residential developments.

Our experienced project teams are supported by dedicated specialists in safety, quality, planning, procurement, design, tendering, billing, finance, human resources and administration—enabling us to deliver reliable solutions tailored to each project.

Project Methodology

Five stages, from GFC review to handover, managed by our own project team.

  1. Stage 01

    Design

    We review the GFC drawings, then prepare shop and GA drawings to site conditions for approval by the PMC or consultant.

  2. Stage 02

    Procurement

    Final quantities go to procurement alongside design. Vendors are validated and material is checked against the BOQ.

  3. Stage 03

    Construction

    We secure the site, stage materials and carry out the works to approved drawings and method statements, under a risk assessment.

  4. Stage 04

    Testing & commissioning

    Functional and visual checks, quality and safety inspections, and test reports from our own commissioning team.

  5. Stage 05

    Handover

    As-built drawings, test reports and warranties. Snags are closed, the client’s team is trained, and a final walkthrough leads to sign-off.

Approvals and long-lead equipment are planned first. Schedule risk is addressed before work begins on site.

Kick-off requirements

  • GFC drawings in hand before the kickoff meeting
  • Samples and technical data sheets approved within 5 days
  • Panel GA and shop drawings approved within one week
  • Ceiling layouts, IT power and raceway needs, and HVAC power loads confirmed up front

Early procurement

  • LT panels
  • Light fixtures
  • Lighting management systems
  • Rising mains and busducts

Order placement, submittals and approvals are closed on a fixed timeline.
