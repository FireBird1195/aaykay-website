What we do

Our work is the electrical package, end to end: containment, cabling, panels, lighting, earthing and critical power. It is coordinated in Revit, installed to written method statements, and tested by our own commissioning team before the client signs off.

Shop drawings & BIM

GFC review, shop and GA drawings, and MEP coordination in Autodesk Revit and AutoCAD.

Testing & commissioning

An in-house team and instrument set for megger, IR, continuity, load and integrated system tests.

Site safety

Dedicated safety staff, HIRA risk assessments and an emergency response team on call 24/7.

How we deliver

Five stages run by one in-house team, with the schedule risks dealt with first.

Electrical packages rarely slip on site. They slip waiting on approvals and long-lead equipment, so that is where we start.

Agreed at kickoff

  • GFC drawings in hand before the kickoff meeting
  • Samples and technical data sheets approved within 5 days
  • Panel GA and shop drawings approved within one week
  • Ceiling layouts, IT power and raceway needs, and HVAC power loads confirmed up front

Ordered early

  • LT panels
  • Light fixtures
  • Lighting management systems
  • Rising mains and busducts

Order placement, submittals and approvals for each are closed on a fixed timeline.

  1. 01

    Design

    GFC drawings reviewed; shop and GA drawings prepared to site conditions and approved by the PMC or consultant.

    • GFC review
    • Shop drawings
    • Submittals
  2. 02

    Procurement

    Final quantities passed to procurement alongside design. Vendors are validated and material is checked against the BOQ.

    • Vendor validation
    • ERP
    • Material tracking
  3. 03

    Construction

    Site secured and materials staged; works executed to approved drawings and method statements under a risk analysis.

    • Site logistics
    • Risk analysis
    • Checklists
  4. 04

    Testing & commissioning

    Functional and visual verification, quality and safety checks, and test reports from our own commissioning team.

    • Megger & IR
    • Panel load
    • System test
  5. 05

    Handover

    As-built drawings, test reports and warranties; snags closed, the client’s team trained, and a final walkthrough to sign-off.

    • As-builts
    • Training
    • Sign-off

What we install

From the electrical works schedule in our profile

Containment

Conduit in slabs, walls and floors; sleeves and back boxes; GI cable trays, ladders, trunking and raceways.

Wiring & cabling

Power and lighting wiring, DB-to-equipment wiring, HT/LT cable laying, dressing, tagging, glanding and termination.

Panels & distribution

LT panels and capacitor banks, MDBs, SMDBs and DBs, control wiring and panel earthing.

Busducts & rising mains

Busbar trunking and rising mains, supplied through OEM channels and installed to approved GA drawings.

Lighting & accessories

LED fixtures, lighting management systems, switches and sockets, fans and junction boxes.

Critical power

UPS and battery installation, server-room electrical works, AC unit connections, pumps and motors.

Earthing & protection

Earth pits and grids, GI and copper strip, surge protection devices and lightning arresters.

Testing

Megger, IR and continuity tests, panel load tests, circuit verification and HT/LT equipment testing.

Quality & safety

Certified to ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018, and run day to day by dedicated quality and safety staff.

Quality

  • QA requirements agreed with the client at the start of each project.
  • A quality control supervisor runs QA checks, reports non-conformities and works with client QA inspectors.
  • Work-type checklists kept current by the site team as execution progresses.
  • Every system tested, commissioned and signed off by the PMC or client.

Safety

  • An emergency response team on call 24/7.
  • Project safety reviews every alternate day, and a weekly safety report reviewed by top management.
  • HIRA risk assessments, height and hot-work permits, and lock-out tag-out before live connections.
  • Toolbox talks, PPE for every worker, monthly medical camps and tie-ups with local hospitals.
