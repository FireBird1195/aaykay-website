AAYKAY Electricals Private Limited is one of the leading ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 certified MEP contracting firms, established in 2008.

With its focus on quality, safety, excellent service, a customer-centric approach, timely completion of projects and technical expertise in design, supply, erection, maintenance, testing and commissioning, AAYKAY has become one of the most preferred MEP contractors with top MNCs and Indian client partners since its inception in 2008.

AAYKAY has completed projects in a wide range of market segments: banks, hospitals, industries, data centres, IT parks, interior fit-outs, substations, and commercial and high-rise residential buildings.

Its site execution team of project managers, project engineers, safety and quality engineers, planning and coordination engineers, supervisors and electricians is supported by tender and marketing, procurement, design and billing engineers, and the accounts, finance, HR and administration team.

Our work

  • All-round electrical services: design, supply, installation, testing and commissioning.

  • Internal and external electrical works, as well as complete MEP packages.

  • Inspection of electrical systems and maintenance of electrical installations, including annual maintenance contracts.

  • Coordination with project management consultants, architects, quantity surveyors, design consultants and real estate service companies.

Project Methodology

Five stages, from GFC review to handover, managed by our own project team.

  1. Stage 01

    Design

    We review the GFC drawings, then prepare shop and GA drawings to site conditions for approval by the PMC or consultant.

  2. Stage 02

    Procurement

    Final quantities go to procurement alongside design. Vendors are validated and material is checked against the BOQ.

  3. Stage 03

    Construction

    We secure the site, stage materials and carry out the works to approved drawings and method statements, under a risk assessment.

  4. Stage 04

    Testing & commissioning

    Functional and visual checks, quality and safety inspections, and test reports from our own commissioning team.

  5. Stage 05

    Handover

    As-built drawings, test reports and warranties. Snags are closed, the client’s team is trained, and a final walkthrough leads to sign-off.

Approvals and long-lead equipment are planned first. Schedule risk is addressed before work begins on site.

Kick-off requirements

  • GFC drawings in hand before the kickoff meeting
  • Samples and technical data sheets approved within 5 days
  • Panel GA and shop drawings approved within one week
  • Ceiling layouts, IT power and raceway needs, and HVAC power loads confirmed up front

Early procurement

  • LT panels
  • Light fixtures
  • Lighting management systems
  • Rising mains and busducts

Order placement, submittals and approvals are closed on a fixed timeline.
