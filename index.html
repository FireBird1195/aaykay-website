Established in 2008, AAYKAY Electricals Private Limited is an ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 certified contractor specialising in electrical and fire safety services. We provide end-to-end expertise in design, supply, installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance.

Our commitment to quality, safety, responsive service and timely project delivery has earned the trust of leading multinational and Indian organisations. We have completed projects across banking, healthcare, manufacturing, data centres, IT parks, interior fit-outs, substations, commercial buildings and high-rise residential developments.

Our experienced project teams are supported by dedicated specialists in safety, quality, planning, procurement, design, tendering, billing, finance, human resources and administration—enabling us to deliver reliable solutions tailored to each project.

Our services

  • Comprehensive Electrical and Fire Safety Services which includes
    End-to-end solutions covering design, supply, installation, testing and commissioning.

  • Inspection and maintenance of electrical installations and fire safety systems, including annual maintenance contracts (AMCs).

  • Effective coordination with PMCs, architects, quantity surveyors, design consultants and builders to support seamless project execution.

  • End-to-end statutory liaising, drawings, and safety clearances from State wise Electricity Boards and CEIG.
    • New LT/HT power sanctions, load enhancements, meter installations, transformer approvals, and name changes.
    • CEIG Approvals: Statutory safety inspections, single-line diagram (SLD) approvals, high-tension (HT) installations, DG sets, and commissioning certification.
