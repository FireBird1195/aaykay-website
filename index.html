What we do

We take on the complete electrical package: containment, cabling, panels, lighting, earthing and critical power. We coordinate it in Revit, install it to written method statements and test it with our own commissioning team before handover.

Shop drawings & BIM

GFC review, shop and GA drawings, and MEP coordination in Autodesk Revit and AutoCAD.

Testing & commissioning

Our own team and test instruments for megger, IR, continuity, load and integrated system tests.

Site safety

Dedicated safety staff, HIRA risk assessments and an emergency response team on call 24/7.

What we install

Containment

Conduit in slabs, walls and floors; sleeves and back boxes; GI cable trays, ladders, trunking and raceways.

Wiring & cabling

Power and lighting wiring, DB-to-equipment wiring, HT/LT cable laying, dressing, tagging, glanding and termination.

Panels & distribution

LT panels and capacitor banks, MDBs, SMDBs and DBs, control wiring and panel earthing.

Busducts & rising mains

Busbar trunking and rising mains, supplied through OEM channels and installed to approved GA drawings.

Lighting & accessories

LED fixtures, lighting management systems, switches and sockets, fans and junction boxes.

Critical power

UPS and battery installation, server-room electrical works, AC unit connections, pumps and motors.

Earthing & protection

Earth pits and grids, GI and copper strip, surge protection devices and lightning arresters.

Testing

Megger, IR and continuity tests, panel load tests, circuit verification and HT/LT equipment testing.

How we deliver

Five stages, managed by our own project team, with the schedule risks dealt with first.

Electrical packages rarely slip on site. They slip waiting on approvals and long-lead equipment, so that is where we start.

Agreed at kickoff

  • GFC drawings in hand before the kickoff meeting
  • Samples and technical data sheets approved within 5 days
  • Panel GA and shop drawings approved within one week
  • Ceiling layouts, IT power and raceway needs, and HVAC power loads confirmed up front

Ordered early

  • LT panels
  • Light fixtures
  • Lighting management systems
  • Rising mains and busducts

Order placement, submittals and approvals for each are closed on a fixed timeline.

  1. Stage 01

    Design

    We review the GFC drawings, then prepare shop and GA drawings to site conditions for approval by the PMC or consultant.

    • GFC review
    • Shop drawings
    • Submittals

  2. Stage 02

    Procurement

    Final quantities go to procurement alongside design. Vendors are validated and material is checked against the BOQ.

    • Vendor validation
    • ERP
    • Material tracking

  3. Stage 03

    Construction

    We secure the site, stage materials and carry out the works to approved drawings and method statements, under a risk assessment.

    • Site logistics
    • Risk analysis
    • Checklists

  4. Stage 04

    Testing & commissioning

    Functional and visual checks, quality and safety inspections, and test reports from our own commissioning team.

    • Megger & IR
    • Panel load
    • System test

  5. Stage 05

    Handover

    As-built drawings, test reports and warranties. Snags are closed, the client’s team is trained, and a final walkthrough leads to sign-off.

    • As-builts
    • Training
    • Sign-off
