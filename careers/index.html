  • Project Engineer – Electrical Project execution Hyderabad 3–6 years Full-time

    Plans and executes electrical works on commercial, healthcare and IT projects, from shop drawings to handover.

    Responsibilities

    • Execute electrical works to approved drawings, method statements and the project schedule
    • Coordinate with the client, PMC, consultants and other services on site
    • Track material, manpower and progress, and report to the project manager
    • Carry out quality checks and support testing and commissioning

    Requirements

    • B.E./B.Tech or diploma in electrical engineering
    • 3–6 years in electrical or MEP site execution
    • Working knowledge of AutoCAD and electrical drawings
    • Sound understanding of site safety practice

  • Site Supervisor – Electrical Project execution Hyderabad / Bengaluru 5+ years Full-time

    Supervises electricians and subcontractors on site, so that work is installed to drawings, safely and on schedule.

    Responsibilities

    • Allocate and supervise daily work of electricians and helpers
    • Check installations against drawings and checklists
    • Maintain site records, material stock and daily progress reports
    • Enforce PPE, permits and barricading on site

    Requirements

    • Diploma or ITI in electrical trade
    • 5 or more years supervising electrical installation
    • Ability to read electrical layouts and single-line diagrams

    Apply now for Site Supervisor – Electrical (opens your email)

  • Safety Officer Safety Hyderabad 2–5 years Full-time

    Runs site safety on AAYKAY projects in line with ISO 45001:2018 and the client’s requirements.

    Responsibilities

    • Conduct safety inductions and toolbox talks
    • Prepare and review HIRA risk assessments and work permits
    • Inspect PPE, scaffolding, barricading and temporary electrical set-ups
    • Report incidents and prepare weekly safety reports

    Requirements

    • Diploma or certificate in industrial safety
    • 2–5 years in construction site safety
    • Working knowledge of ISO 45001 and statutory safety requirements

    Apply now for Safety Officer (opens your email)

  • Design Engineer – Electrical Design Hyderabad 2–4 years Full-time

    Prepares shop drawings, GA drawings and MEP coordination models for AAYKAY projects.

    Responsibilities

    • Prepare shop and GA drawings from GFC drawings
    • Coordinate MEP services in Autodesk Revit
    • Prepare quantity take-offs and technical submittals
    • Update as-built drawings at handover

    Requirements

    • B.E./B.Tech in electrical engineering
    • 2–4 years in electrical design or drafting
    • Proficiency in AutoCAD and Autodesk Revit
